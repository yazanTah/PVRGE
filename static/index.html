How FlowPurger Works

Problem 1: C2PA & JUMBF Manifests

Google Flow & Veo videos embed a cryptographically signed c2pa manifest inside MP4 container boxes (specifically uuid and JUMBF boxes). Social platforms like TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube read these exact bytes on upload and automatically slap mandatory "AI-generated" warnings or suppress algorithm reach.

The Fix (Quick Clean): FlowPurger performs a bitexact container remux that strips 100% of C2PA manifests, XMP packets, and Google encoder tags without recompressing video or audio streams. Duration: ~0.08s with 0.00% quality loss.

Problem 2: DeepMind SynthID Watermark

Google DeepMind's SynthID embeds an imperceptible watermark directly into the pixel color frequencies of video frames. Unlike C2PA, this survives container metadata stripping because it lives inside the image pixels themselves.

The Fix (Deep Clean): FlowPurger applies a specialized micro-temporal dither and perceptual contrast shift that scatters the statistical pixel correlation required by SynthID detectors to verify watermarks. It then re-encodes the stream with high-fidelity x264 (CRF 18), completely breaking automated detection while keeping the visual output identical to human viewers.
